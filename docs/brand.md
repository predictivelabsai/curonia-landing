# Curonia Capital brand guidelines

## Positioning

Curonia Capital Ltd is a senior-led M&A advisory boutique for the Baltics and Central and Eastern Europe. It advises owners, investors and boards on sales, acquisitions and strategic options, sharpened by a proprietary deal-intelligence platform built on LiquidRound. Technology prepares; people judge.

Brand idea: **Quiet precision.** The name refers to the Curonian Spit, the narrow line of dune between the Baltic Sea and the lagoon. Baltic amber is the accent material.

Audience: founders and families, private equity and family offices, corporate boards. They value discretion, seniority and clear judgement over volume or hype.

## Messaging pillars

1. **Senior-led discretion.** A senior adviser leads every mandate; we accept a limited number.
2. **Proprietary intelligence.** Our platform, built on LiquidRound, maps buyers and targets, assembles comparables and drafts materials.
3. **Judgement stays human.** Advisers verify every output and own every recommendation.
4. **Baltic roots, CEE reach.** Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia, with a cross-border view of Poland and the wider region, advised from London.

## Voice and tone

Restrained, precise, warm without being casual. Short sentences. British English. No em dashes.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "We take on a deliberately small number of mandates." | "We're the leading M&A powerhouse in the region!" |
| "Advisers verify every output." | "Our revolutionary AI unlocks game-changing insights." |
| "Clear recommendations, including when the right answer is to wait." | "Maximise your exit value today." |

Never state deal counts, transaction values, client names, rankings or credentials that have not been verified and approved.

## Typography

- **Newsreader** (Light 300, Regular 400, Italic): headlines, statements, names. Italic amber is reserved for one emphasised word per headline.
- **Geist** (400, 500): body copy and interface.
- **Geist Mono** (400): small uppercase labels, indices and numerals, tracked at 0.14 to 0.16em.

Fluid scale from `--step--1` to `--step-5` in `static/site.css`. Headlines sit at tight leading (1.02 to 1.1) with generous space around them.

## Logo

- **Mark:** an open arc forming a "C" and the curve of the spit, crossed by a fine amber horizon line.
- **Lockup:** mark with CURONIA in widely tracked serif capitals over CAPITAL in small mono capitals.
- Files: `static/brand/curonia-mark.svg`, `curonia-lockup.svg` (light grounds), `curonia-lockup-reverse.svg` (night ground), `static/favicon.svg`, `static/apple-touch-icon.png`, `static/og-image.png`.
- Clear space: at least the height of the mark's opening on every side. Minimum size: mark 16px, lockup 120px wide.
- Do not recolour the horizon line, add effects or shadows, stretch, rotate or set the wordmark in another typeface.

## Imagery and motion

- Fine-line motifs: contour lines of the spit, sparse networks linking London and the Baltic capitals, dot fields on night grounds. Abstract, never illustrative product screens or stock photography.
- People are shown in black and white portraits on a consistent crop (about 800 x 910 px).
- Motion is slow and quiet: lines draw once, amber points pulse gently, content rises a few pixels into view. Everything respects reduced-motion preferences and pauses off screen.
